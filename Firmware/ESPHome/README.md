# HomeAssistant Tutorial
[![Home Assistant](https://img.shields.io/badge/Home_Assistant-YAML-41BDF5?logo=homeassistant&logoColor=white.svg)](https://www.home-assistant.io/)

**1** Go to you HomeAssistant. Open the "SETTINGS" and click on "APPS".<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/1.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 1" width="800"/><br><br>

**2** Click on "You dont have any apps installed yet..."<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/2.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 2" width="800"/><br><br>

**3** Type in "ESPHome" and click (install) the "ESPHome device builder"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/3.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 3" width="800"/><br><br>

**4** Install ESPHome by clicking the Botton <br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/4.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 4" width="800"/><br><br>

**5** Start the ESOHome by clicking "START"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/5.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 5" width="800"/><br><br>

**6** Click "OPEN WEB UI"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/6.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 6" width="800"/><br><br>

**7** Add a new Device by clicking the "+" Icon<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/7.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 7" width="800"/><br><br>

**8** Open the "ADVANCED SETUP OPTION" and click on "EMPTY CONFIGURATION"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/8.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 8" width="800"/><br><br>

**9** Put in any name it does not matter because that will be overwritten later. Continue by clicking "FINISH SETUP"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/9.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 9" width="800"/><br><br>

**10** Go to the Yaml file here on Github (new TAB) and copy that to the clipboard by clicking the "COPY"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/10.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 10" width="800"/><br><br>

**11** Go back to the configuration. Select and delete the whole text<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/11.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 11" width="800"/><br><br>

**12** Paste the copied text from yout clipboard bei right click and choose paste (or STRG+V or CMD+V)<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/12.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 12" width="800"/><br><br>

**13** Gp to WIFI and put in your WIFI Credentials<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/13.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 13" width="800"/><br><br>

**14** Click on SAVE (right bottom corner). Then click install and Choose "PLUG INTO THIS COMPUTER" **THIS MAY TAKE UP TO 20 MINUTES**<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/14.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 14" width="800"/><br><br>

**15** "OPEN USB FLASHER"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/15.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 15" width="800"/><br><br>

**16** Choose "CONNECT AND INSTALL"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/16.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 16" width="800"/><br><br>

**17** Search for your USB COM Port. Usually there is only one in the list. And click on "CONNECT" **THIS MAY TAKE SOME TIME TOO.** If there opens a (LOG) window, you can close this tab and go back to your HomeAssistant<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/17.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 17" width="800"/><br><br>

**18** Go to "OVERVIEW" and click on "DEVICES DISCOVERED"<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/18.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 18" width="800"/><br><br>

**19** You should find **The Airflow Project** in the List. Click on it.<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/19.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 19" width="800"/><br><br>

**20** On "DEVICES" you should find the controls<br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/20.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 20" width="800"/><br><br>

**21** DONE. Now you can assign **The AirFlow Project** to any area you want <br>
<img src="/Images/ESPHome_Tutorial/21.jpg" alt="ESPHome step 21" width="800"/><br><br>
