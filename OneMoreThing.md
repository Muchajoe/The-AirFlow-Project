# One More Thing...

Every carefully routed trace on this compact hub was designed with a single, primary goal in mind: mastering thermals. It orchestrates signals, tames the heat, and keeps the breeze flowing exactly as intended.

But hardware is only as rigid as the firmware that drives it. 

Hidden within the core of this controller lies an alternative path. If you are willing to strip away the fans, push a new payload, and completely reassign its purpose, the logic of cooling gives way to a completely different spectrum. 

*If the airflow settles, there could be room for something brighter.*
